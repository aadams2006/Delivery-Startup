Our Story

We are architects of the new aerial commons.

Hovr blends aviation, autonomy, and urban design expertise to build a delivery network that respects cities and the people who live in them.

Mission

Liberate streets. Elevate service.

Our mission is to reimagine logistics around the human scale — fewer vans at the curb, more breathable neighborhoods, and access to essentials in minutes.

Values

We lead with responsibility

Safety first, communities at the core, sustainability by default, and delight in every interaction.
